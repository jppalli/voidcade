How to play

  • Divide the whole grid into rectangles.
  • Each rectangle must contain exactly one number.
  • That number equals the area (cell count) of the rectangle.
  • Click and drag to draw a rectangle, release to lock it in.
  • Tap a locked region to remove it if you change your mind.
  • Fill the entire board to win.
  • Each day has 3 levels — Easy, Medium, Hard. Clear all 3 to complete the day and keep your streak.
  • Missed a day? Browse back with the arrows and play any past puzzle.
