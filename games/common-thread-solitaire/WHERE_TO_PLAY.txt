┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ GAMEPLAY                                                        │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

1. DRAG a card from stock area
2. DROP on any slot
3. When 4 MATCHING cards fill a slot → ✓ COMPLETE!
   (Arpeggio sound plays, category revealed)
4. Complete all 3 sets → 🏆 WIN!


┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ CONTROLS                                                        │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

Mouse/Touch:  Drag cards to move them
Ctrl+Z:       Undo last move
H:            Show hint (highlights 2 matching cards)
Ctrl+R:       Restart level

┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ TROUBLESHOOTING                                                 │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

Q: No sound?
A: Click sound button (top-right) or tap anywhere first

Q: Cards won't drag?
A: Check browser console (F12) for JavaScript errors

Q: Want to reset progress?
A: Browser console → localStorage.clear() → Reload

Q: Port already in use?
A: Change port in vite.config.ts or kill existing process
