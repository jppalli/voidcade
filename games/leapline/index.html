How to Play

Place the numbers 1 through the last number on the grid, in order. Each number must land 1, 2, or 3 cells away from the previous one — straight across a row or column, never diagonally.

Tap the glowing cells to see where the next number can legally go. The dots on each glowing cell show the exact distance (• = 1, •• = 2, ••• = 3).

  • Every puzzle has exactly one valid solution — no guessing games with multiple right answers.
  • A wrong-but-legal tap is tracked as a mistake, but nothing is lost. Try another cell.
  • Hint reveals a little more each time you ask: first the distance, then the direction, then the exact cell.
  • Undo removes your last placed number. Restart clears the whole board back to the start.
