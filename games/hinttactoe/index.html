How to Play

It's tic-tac-toe, but every square has a price. Each row has a category, and each column has a trait. To claim a square you must answer with something that fits both.

Example: row "Wild Animal" × column "Can Fly" → you could answer eagle, bat, or owl.

  • Tap an empty square, type your answer, and submit it.
  • Correct answer → your mark (X or O) is placed and the turn passes.
  • Wrong or unrecognized answer → no mark is placed and the turn passes anyway.
  • Get three marks in a row, column, or diagonal to win. Fill the board with no winner and it's a draw.
  • Play vs Computer pits you (X) against an AI (O) that always plays a smart square and always answers correctly — outsmart its board position, not its vocabulary.

Answers are checked against a curated word list per square, so spelling matters more than trivia — plurals and minor punctuation are forgiven.
