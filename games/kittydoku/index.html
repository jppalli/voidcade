How to Play

  • 1Place exactly one cat in every row, every column, and every colour patch.
  • 2No two cats will sit next to each other — not side by side, not corner to corner. The faded paws above show the squares those two cats rule out.
  • 3Tap a square once for a paw mark (your note for "no cat here"), again to place a cat, again to clear it.

Every puzzle has exactly one answer and can always be worked out — no guessing, no timer, and nothing to lose.
