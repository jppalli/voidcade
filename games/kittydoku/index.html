How to Play

  • 1Place exactly one cat in every row, every column, and every colour patch.
  • 2No two cats will sit next to each other — not side by side, not corner to corner. The faded paws above show the squares those two cats rule out.
  • 3Tap a square to place a cat. If you're wrong the cat looks sad, you lose a heart, and the cell stays revealed. Three hearts per level.

Every puzzle has exactly one answer and can always be worked out by logic — no guessing needed.
