How to Play

  1. 1 One of eachPlace exactly one cat in every row, every column and every colour patch.
  2. 2 No touchingCats never sit next to each other, not side by side and not corner to corner.
  3. 3 Paws are free notesTap a square to leave a paw meaning "no cat here", or hold and drag to paint paws. Tap a paw again to clear it.
  4. 4 Place a catDouble-tap a square when you're sure. A right square and the cat settles in. A wrong one costs a heart.

Every puzzle has exactly one answer and can always be worked out by logic. No guessing needed.
