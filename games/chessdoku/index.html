How to Play

Place every piece from the tray onto the board so that no piece attacks another — the fixed purple pieces included.

  • ♛Queen — attacks rows, columns & diagonals
  • ♜Rook — attacks along rows & columns
  • ♝Bishop — attacks along diagonals
  • ♞Knight — jumps in an L, leaps over blockers
  • ♚King — attacks the 8 touching squares
  • ♟Pawn — attacks the 2 squares diagonally above it

Sliding pieces stop at the first piece or cracked square in their path — walls can shield pieces from each other.

Some levels add sudoku rules: exactly one piece per row and column, or one piece per outlined box. Fixed purple pieces are givens — they cannot be moved.

Click a tray piece to select it, click a square to place it, click a placed piece to take it back.
