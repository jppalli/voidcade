How to play

  1. Guess the hidden word or phrase one letter at a time.
  2. Each wrong letter adds a line to the drawing. Six misses and the round is over.
  3. Each level is one topic with five words, and every word gets a fresh drawing.

Fewer misses across the level earn more stars. In Endless, keep solving to build your score.
